The Philosophy

A Taste of
Togetherness

Rooted in the timeless Indian tradition of Atithi Devo Bhava, Aashri Pure Veg Restaurant & Banquet was conceived as a destination where families gather, milestones are commemorated, and vegetarian cuisine is elevated to an art form.

From the terracotta-hued architectural arches and warm ambient chandeliers to the meticulous hand of our culinary brigade, every detail is crafted to provide Nagpur with an immersive, heartfelt dining experience.

100% Pure Vegetarian

Strictly vegetarian kitchen adhering to highest purity, fresh ingredients, and Jain options.

Multi-Cuisine Craft

North Indian specialties, tandoori delicacies, Indo-Chinese favorites, and continental platters.

Luxury Banquet Floor

Grand air-conditioned celebration hall with crystal chandeliers for private functions.

Inclusive & Accessible

Elevator-equipped, wheelchair-friendly entrance and seating, with valet parking convenience.
