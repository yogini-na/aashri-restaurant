Our Heritage & Philosophy

Hospitality Grounded in
Warmth & Tradition

Rooted in the timeless Indian spirit of Atithi Devo Bhava, Aashri was founded to provide Nagpur families with a pristine, celebratory dining environment where pure vegetarian cuisine is prepared with genuine passion.

From hand-crafted terracotta arches and ambient cove lighting to spacious banquet arrangements, every space is designed to make your family dinners, anniversaries, and festive celebrations unforgettable.

Pure Vegetarian Kitchen

Strictly vegetarian kitchen with fresh farm ingredients and authentic Jain menu preparation.

Multi-Cuisine Expertise

Clay-oven tandoori starters, rich North Indian curries, Indo-Chinese delicacies, and continental platters.

Grand Banquet Hall

Fully air-conditioned luxury ballroom with crystal chandeliers for engagements and birthdays.

Complete Accessibility

High-speed passenger elevator, wheelchair-accessible dining seating, and valet assistance.
