Frequently Asked Questions

Guest Information

Helpful answers regarding our pure vegetarian dining and banquet bookings.

Is Aashri strictly 100% pure vegetarian? +
Yes, absolutely. Aashri Pure Veg Restaurant & Banquet maintains a strictly 100% vegetarian kitchen with zero eggs or meat products. We also prepare dedicated Jain food preparations (no onion, garlic, or root vegetables) across our starters, paneer specialties, gravies, and dals.
How can I book the banquet hall for a family event? +
You can fill out our interactive Banquet Enquiry form on this website or call our banquet desk directly at +91 88068 59456. Our banquet manager will check calendar availability, discuss your expected guest count, and customize a bespoke pure vegetarian buffet menu with live food counters.
Is the venue accessible for senior citizens and wheelchairs? +
Yes, our premises at Mangalmurti Square are fully equipped with smooth ramp access, a high-capacity passenger elevator directly serving all dining and banquet floors, wide aisle corridors, and wheelchair-accessible restrooms.
Do you offer parking facilities? +
Yes, we provide dedicated parking assistance and valet services at Mangalmurti Square to ensure you and your banquet guests have a hassle-free arrival.
Do you accept reservations for large family dinners? +
Yes! For family gatherings and parties of 4 or more, we strongly recommend reserving in advance via our website or telephone (+91 88068 59456) so we can prepare your preferred booth seating or table configuration.
